## Description
Finora is a personal finance audit. Add your bank and card statements, receipts and cash entries, and Finora shows what you earned, where the money went and what is coming next month.

- Overview and Explore: spending by category, merchant and month, down to the single transaction.
- Import: read a statement PDF or a photo of a receipt, then check what was found.
- Inbox: Finora asks about anything unclear, such as an unconfirmed deposit, instead of guessing.
- Forecast and recurring bills: what repeats, what is due and how next month looks.
- Reports: save and share a PDF of any period.
- Ask: type a question about your money and get an answer built from your own ledger.

Card payments and moves between your own accounts are never counted as spending or income. Business and personal spending can be viewed apart.

Finora does not move money, connect to your bank, or give investment advice.
