# Finora — logo asset package

The product name is **Finora**. All updated wordmark and full-logo SVG/PNG assets spell **Finora**. The original finance-control logo mark (roof outline, bar chart, and rising indigo arrow) and color palette are retained.

## Contents
- `01_symbol_color` — color roof + financial chart + indigo arrow symbol.
- `02–03_symbol_monochrome_*` — charcoal and white standalone symbol.
- `04_symbol_reversed` — white + indigo symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `05–06_wordmark_*` — outlined **Finora** lettering on a transparent background, dark and white versions.
- `07–08_tagline_*` — “YOUR FINANCES. IN CONTROL.” dark and white versions.
- `09_logo_horizontal_color` — full color logo with Finora + tagline, transparent background.
- `10_logo_horizontal_reversed` — full reversed logo, transparent background.
- `11_logo_horizontal_monochrome` — monochrome logo with Finora + tagline.
- `12–13_app_icon_*` — app icons with rounded square light/dark backgrounds (symbol only).
- `14_favicon_transparent` — transparent symbol, multiple sizes.
- `15–18_feature_*` — track expenses, manage income, plan goals and smarter decisions symbols.
- `Finora_Assets_Preview.png` — visual index of the brand assets.

## Formats
SVG logos have path-outlined lettering, so no font installation is required. Standalone logos have transparent backgrounds. PNGs are premultiplied-free RGBA exports. Lettered wordmarks and horizontal lockups are approximately proportioned to their revised width.

## Reproduction notes
These are traced and recreated vectors from the supplied image, not the original designer's editable source files. To update the name, the original glyph contours for F, n, o, r and a were kept, the e was replaced with an outlined i, and the remaining letters and tagline were repositioned. The supporting financial pictograms are illustrative redraws. Symbol-only app icons were unchanged by the name update.
